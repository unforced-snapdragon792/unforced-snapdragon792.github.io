## 🚀 Setting up the application

You need to configure your workspace after the installation. Open the Conformiti application from your desktop or start menu. 

The first screen asks for your organization details. Enter your company name and choose your region. This ensures the app applies the correct security frameworks to your audits. You can skip this step, but setting it up now saves time later.

Next, you will link your cloud accounts. Conformiti pulls data from your services to check against security rules. Select the services you want to track from the list. The app guides you through the sign-in process for each service. 

## 📋 Managing your first audit

Audit management involves three phases. Use the dashboard to track your progress at each stage.

### Phase 1: Planning
Create a new audit project. Select the standard you want to meet, such as SOC2 or ISO 27001. The software outlines the requirements for that standard. Assign tasks to your team members so everyone knows their responsibilities.

### Phase 2: Evidence collection
Security audits require proof that your controls function correctly. Conformiti prompts you to upload files or connect software logs. The app checks these logs against the standards you selected. It marks requirements as complete when it finds the evidence.

### Phase 3: Reporting
The system creates reports once you collect all evidence. Download these reports as PDFs to share with your customers or auditors. The reports show exactly where you stand and what gaps remain in your security.

## 💡 Common tasks

### Updating the software
Conformiti checks for updates when you start the program. It alerts you if a newer version exists. Click the update button to install the latest patches. This keeps your compliance data safe and unlocks new audit frameworks.

### Creating custom controls
Standard frameworks cover many basics, but your product might need specific security rules. Open the settings menu and select Manage Controls. You can write your own requirements here. Link these custom controls to your audits to track them alongside global standards.

### Scheduling scans
You should run security checks often. Go to the Automation tab to set a schedule. Choose a time for the app to scan your cloud accounts. You receive a notification if the scan finds a configuration change that creates a security risk.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

### Does this app share my data?
All your data stays on your local machine. Conformiti does not send your private audit files to a central server. You control your information at all times.

### Can I export my data?
Yes. Use the Export button in the main menu to save your audit history as a CSV file. Use this file in other programs if you need to provide data to legal teams.

### What if the app fails to open?
Check that your antivirus program does not block the software. Some settings flag new applications as suspicious. Add Conformiti to your list of trusted apps if your security software blocks it.

### Do I need to be a developer?
No. Every feature focuses on compliance experts and project managers. You do not need to read code or write scripts to manage your audits.

### How do I remove the app?
Open your Windows Settings and choose Apps and Features. Find Conformiti in the list and click Uninstall. This removes the program from your system. It does not delete your saved audit files unless you choose to delete them separately.
